I am a PhD student at [ITU](https://www.itu.edu.tr). My advisor is [Atabey Kaygun](https://web.itu.edu.tr/kaygun/). I obtained my B.S., and M.S. degrees in mathematics from [MSGSU](https://msgsu.edu.tr) under the supervision of [Özer Öztürk](http://mat.msgsu.edu.tr/~ozer/).

My research interest lies in homological and abstract homotopical algebra. I am mainly interested in understanding model categories and Quillen equivalences on crossed simplicial groups and corresponding chain complexes. I am also interested in algebraic topology, algebraic geometry, higher category theory and related areas.

Alongside my theoretical work, I have recently been expanding my expertise in statistical learning, machine learning, and deep learning. I am particularly interested in the mathematical foundations of learning theory and in exploring potential connections between categorical, homological, and statistical perspectives in modern machine learning.
